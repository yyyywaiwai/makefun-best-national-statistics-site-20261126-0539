Economy

Japan is the world's fourth-largest economy. Nominal GDP reached ¥609.3 trillion in 2024 (about $4.0 trillion), after the economy returned to growth in 2023 following the pandemic contraction.

The pandemic cut real GDP by 4.1% in 2020. Nominal GDP passed ¥600 trillion for the first time in 2024, helped by price rises as much as by volume growth.

Reading the economy

Three things shape Japan's economy today. First, consumption is the engine but it is soft: real wages only began to rise again in 2024 after two years of falling behind prices. Second, the yen's weakness since 2022 has lifted exporters' profits and the nominal value of exports, while making energy imports expensive. Third, public debt is about 250% of GDP — the highest among advanced economies — so fiscal space is limited even as defence and child-care spending rise.
