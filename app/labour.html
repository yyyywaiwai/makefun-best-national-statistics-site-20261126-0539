Labour market

Japan's unemployment rate has stayed near 2.5% for years — low by any standard — but the headline hides a tight market: there are more open jobs than job-seekers, and a third of workers are part-time.

Unemployment and the labour force, 2019–2025

Unemployment rate, % (line); labour force, millions (bars). Source: Statistics Bureau of Japan, Labour Force Survey (annual averages).

Unemployment spiked to 2.8% in the pandemic years and has settled at 2.5%. The labour force kept growing even as the population shrank, because more women and older people work: the employment rate for women is now about 78%, and for people aged 65–69 about 52%.

Average monthly wages, 2019–2025

Average cash earnings per worker, yen per month. Source: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, Monthly Labour Survey.

Nominal pay is rising; real pay only just caught up. The 2025 spring wage round (shuntō) delivered base-pay rises of about 5.4% — the largest in 34 years — but two years of inflation above 3% had already eroded purchasing power. Real wages returned to year-on-year growth in 2025.
