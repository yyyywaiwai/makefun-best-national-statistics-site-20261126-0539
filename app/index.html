Population is shrinking — slowly

Total population, 2019–2025, millions. Source: Statistics Bureau of Japan, Population Estimates (1 Oct each year).

What to take from this: Japan lost about 2.4 million people between 2019 and 2025. The working-age population is falling faster than the total, which is why the age structure on the Population page matters more than the headline.
